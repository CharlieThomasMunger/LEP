Writes on school placement, guardianship and family decisions in Luxembourg and Switzerland. --- James Liu (Chinese: 刘一卜) is the founder of Lux Education Partners, a Luxembourg-based practice supporting international families through school placement, host family and guardianship coordination. He has more than twenty years of experience in international education and lives in Luxembourg with his family. He works in Chinese with families and in English with schools. ## What he writes about - Placing a child in school in Luxembourg, including cases where no parent is resident - Guardianship and host family arrangements, and how school policies differ - Swiss boarding schools and Swiss hotel management programmes - The costs, timelines and trade-offs families weigh when they have more than one option ## How the guides are written Every guide states what the answer rests on: government regulation, written confirmation from a school, observed cases in practice, or his own professional judgment. These are marked separately so a reader can tell the difference. No guide promises an admission outcome. Admissions decisions are made solely by the school. ## Contact contact@luxedupartners.lu Lux Education Partners is operated by Silver Bay Mountain Group S.à r.l., Luxembourg (RCS B293621).
