I live and operate businesses in Luxembourg, with long-term business relationships with schools and enterprises in Switzerland, the Netherlands and elsewhere in Europe. When I visit a school, I look beyond the campus: which resources can a student actually use, where can their education lead, and how does that connect with future work?

Direct conversations and in-depth interviews with principals and school owners at our partner schools inform my understanding of their educational approach and resources. LEP considers school choice, family budgets, local living arrangements and longer-term development together.
