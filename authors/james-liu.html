James Liu and 修印先生 are the same person: the Luxembourg-based founder of Lux Education Partners (LEP). 修印先生 is his Chinese publishing name.

I live and operate businesses in Luxembourg, with long-term business relationships with schools and enterprises in Switzerland, the Netherlands and elsewhere in Europe. When I visit a school, I look beyond the campus: which resources can a student actually use, where can their education lead, and how does that connect with future work?

Direct conversations and in-depth interviews with principals and school owners at our partner schools inform my understanding of their educational approach and resources. LEP considers school choice, family budgets, local living arrangements and longer-term development together.

James Liu writes in Chinese as 修印先生.

How LEP supports families

Lux Education Partners (LEP) is a Luxembourg-based European education services brand operated by Silver Bay Mountain Group S.à r.l., registered under RCS B293621. Its website is luxedupartners.lu.

LEP works directly with students and families on Luxembourg school applications and host-family arrangements, international boarding schools in the Netherlands and Switzerland, and Swiss undergraduate and postgraduate hospitality education. Support starts before arrival, with application documents, school liaison and coordination of documents required for student visa applications, and continues through the agreed arrival and local arrangements.
