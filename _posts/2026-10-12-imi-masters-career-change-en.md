<h2>What the MSc teaches</h2><p>The programme is validated by Manchester Metropolitan University in the UK. It covers hospitality operations and assets, international strategy, people, concept development, digital marketing and research, followed by a dissertation.</p><p>I am James Liu. A career change should begin with the work you want to do. Analysing a hotel business, running operations and working in a professional kitchen require different preparation.</p>

<h2>MSc or Graduate Diploma?</h2><p>For a master’s degree, consider the MSc. IMI’s hospitality and culinary Graduate Diplomas are professional diplomas with 22-week study and optional internship arrangements. They address a different aim.</p><p>Someone needing a postgraduate degree for further applications should assess the MSc qualification. A restaurant operator wanting practical kitchen training should compare the culinary content directly. Purpose determines qualification and duration.</p>
