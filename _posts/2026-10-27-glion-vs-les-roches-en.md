<h2>Compare the same level and location</h2>
<p>The starting comparison is Glion’s Swiss Bachelor in International Hospitality Business and Les Roches’ Swiss Bachelor in Global Hospitality Management. Both belong to Sommet Education. A master, transfer-entry offer or Spanish campus has a different budget.</p>
<div class="table-scroll"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Item</th><th scope="col">Glion</th><th scope="col">Les Roches</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Usual starting route</td><td>Experience year + three-year bachelor</td><td>Experience year + three-year bachelor</td></tr><tr><td>2027 listed components</td><td>About CHF 232,200</td><td>About CHF 226,400, summing components</td></tr><tr><td>Later housing added in our model</td><td>CHF 12,600 for two periods</td><td>CHF 12,640 for two periods</td></tr><tr><td>Practice and career experience</td><td>Two internships plus further professional experience</td><td>Two internships; final options include Launch Your Career</td></tr><tr><td>Swiss setting</td><td>Glion and Bulle, according to study stage</td><td>Crans-Montana mountain resort</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
<p>The listed difference is around CHF 5,800, modest relative to the whole investment. Scholarships, credit recognition, room choice and the final stage can change it. Start with <a href="/en/insights/swiss-hospitality-bachelor-total-cost/">the complete cost model including internship savings</a>.</p>

<h2>What does “more practical” mean for this student?</h2>
<p>A student we met at Glion described choosing its practical, frontline approach after comparing other schools. That is useful lived feedback. Les Roches also begins with practical training, restaurant operations and service tasks. Neither school can sensibly be reduced to only theory or only practice.</p>
<div class="table-scroll"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Interest</th><th scope="col">Swiss bachelor content to examine</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Luxury and customer experience</td><td>Glion lists Luxury Brand Strategy; its separate Luxury Business bachelor needs a separate comparison.</td></tr><tr><td>Assets and finance</td><td>Glion: Asset Development & Finance. Les Roches: Hotel Financial Performance Management.</td></tr><tr><td>Events</td><td>Glion: Guest Experience in Event Management.</td></tr><tr><td>Entrepreneurship and marketing</td><td>Les Roches: Innovation and Venture Creation; Digital Marketing Strategies.</td></tr><tr><td>Luxury hotel operations</td><td>Les Roches: Luxury Hospitality Management, alongside the hotel-business foundations at both schools.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
<p>LEP connects a preferred role to courses, projects and internships. An interest in luxury might mean brand marketing, sales, customer relationships or hotel operations; those are different plans.</p>

<h2>Les Roches made the strongest overall impression on me</h2>
<p>Of the Swiss hospitality schools on this trip, Les Roches gave me the best overall visit experience. Preparation, student reception and the schedule were thorough. Over roughly three and a half hours we explored residences, teaching and activity spaces. The food, range of dishes and desserts stood out.</p>
<p>Crans-Montana is a mountain resort; the scenery and sports spaces will appeal to students who value outdoor life. We met bachelor and postgraduate students and discussed internships, employment and entrepreneurship with the careers team.</p>
<p>Glion also arranged a professional visit, with academic and internship staff and both Swiss campuses. The mountain setting, restaurant, accommodation and activity spaces impressed us; Bulle offers a different town environment. It is another town, not simply the lower floor of one hillside campus. I use these observations alongside the curriculum and assessment arrangements.</p>

<h2>Where are the other campuses?</h2>
<div class="table-scroll"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">School</th><th scope="col">Locations listed by the school</th><th scope="col">What changes</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Glion</td><td>Glion and Bulle in Switzerland; London in the UK</td><td>Study arrangements, housing, fees and employment setting.</td></tr><tr><td>Les Roches</td><td>Crans-Montana, Switzerland; Marbella, Spain; Abu Dhabi, UAE</td><td>Available programmes, specialisations, intake and local fees.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
<p>Les Roches also lists its partner Indian School of Hospitality in Gurugram; distinguish a partner institution from a direct campus. A family interested in Spain should examine Les Roches Marbella, not a Glion Spanish campus. Swiss fees and Swiss internship wages should not be carried over to another country.</p>

<h2>Transfers can be possible</h2>
<p>Glion’s 2027 terms provide for permanent transfers to other Glion or Les Roches campuses subject to the receiving institution’s normal admission requirements and capacity. The final-year institution matters for the degree awarded. An exchange and a permanent transfer are different arrangements.</p>
<p>LEP can check curriculum progress, eligible semester, grades, fees and scholarship continuity. The shared group offers possibilities, but the initial campus and course still deserve careful selection.</p>
