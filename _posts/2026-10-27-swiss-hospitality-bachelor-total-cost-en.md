<h2>Start with the whole route</h2>
<p>A three-year bachelor, an experience year followed by a bachelor, and admission with transferred credits are different budgets. This comparison assumes entry at the beginning, without a scholarship. It uses the 2027 SEG, Glion and Les Roches schedules and IMI’s current published prices, held constant for calculation; future increases need a separate allowance. All amounts below are CHF.</p>
<div class="table-scroll"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">School / route</th><th scope="col">Tuition</th><th scope="col">Listed room + meals</th><th scope="col">Other listed charges</th><th scope="col">Listed total CHF</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>IMI<br><small>Hospitality business bachelor, about 3 years</small></td><td>99,000</td><td>17,850</td><td>6,000</td><td>122,850</td></tr><tr><td>HIM Business School<br><small>Business bachelor, 3 years</small></td><td>81,000</td><td>24,600</td><td>26,200</td><td>131,800</td></tr><tr><td>César Ritz Colleges<br><small>Industry experience year + 3-year bachelor</small></td><td>88,000</td><td>41,400</td><td>39,900</td><td>169,300</td></tr><tr><td>SHMS<br><small>Hospitality bachelor, 3 years</small></td><td>130,700</td><td>27,200</td><td>28,600</td><td>186,500</td></tr><tr><td>Glion<br><small>Experience year + 3-year bachelor</small></td><td>176,500</td><td>35,000</td><td>20,675</td><td>232,175</td></tr><tr><td>Les Roches<br><small>Experience year + 3-year bachelor; final Launch Your Career</small></td><td>169,875</td><td>42,550</td><td>13,975</td><td>226,400</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
<p>HIM, César Ritz and SHMS are part of SEG; Glion and Les Roches belong to Sommet Education. They offer different programmes. Culinary Arts Academy is a separate culinary/food-service choice, with listed 2027 whole-course charges of about CHF 176,200.</p>

<h2>What accommodation does the table include?</h2>
<ul><li>IMI: three 22-week academic periods, each with tuition 33,000, registration 2,000, standard twin room 3,000 and full meals 2,950: total 122,850. A single room adds the relevant difference.</li><li>The SEG totals follow each complete bachelor fee schedule. Adding meals already included in the schedule would count them twice.</li><li>Glion and Les Roches list accommodation only for some teaching periods. The next model adds two later study periods of housing; internship living costs are deducted separately from internship earnings.</li><li>Les Roches uses the final Launch Your Career option. Remaining on campus for Academic Deep Dive adds academic and living costs.</li><li>Glion’s meal figures are the listed prepaid amounts; Les Roches also has meal arrangements for later off-campus study. Upgrades, extra meals and holiday stays require further budgeting.</li></ul>

<h2>Use internship savings, not gross pay</h2>
<p>For eligible Swiss hospitality internships covered by Article 11 of the sector collective agreement, the 2027 minimum is CHF 2,404 gross monthly. Six fully paid months produce 14,424 gross; twelve produce 28,848. Other countries and later internship years require their own applicable contract and rates.</p>
<div class="table-scroll"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">12 paid months: combined monthly deductions and living costs</th><th scope="col">Gross earnings</th><th scope="col">Total deductions and living costs</th><th scope="col">Amount retained</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>1,500</td><td>28,848</td><td>18,000</td><td>10,848</td></tr><tr><td>1,800</td><td>28,848</td><td>21,600</td><td>7,248</td></tr><tr><td>2,100</td><td>28,848</td><td>25,200</td><td>3,648</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
<p>The central example retains CHF 7,248. Do not deduct employer-provided room and meals twice if they are already taken from pay. A qualifying thirteenth salary is additional under the contract and rules. Internship length and location must be replaced with the student’s actual arrangements.</p>

<h2>A comparable planning model</h2>
<p>We add two later housing periods at the 2027 reference room rates: 12,600 for Glion and 12,640 for Les Roches. Then we allow 5,000–10,000 per programme year for unlisted study-period spending, holidays, flights and insurance where relevant. These are planning inputs, not school quotations, and exclude living costs already deducted during internships.</p>
<p>The following model subtracts the same 7,248 retained over twelve paid internship months. Additional placements or longer professional phases need their own earnings and expenses.</p>
<div class="table-scroll"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">School</th><th scope="col">Later study-period housing added</th><th scope="col">Whole-course extra allowance</th><th scope="col">Modelled family net cost CHF</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>IMI</td><td>0</td><td>15,000–30,000</td><td>130,602–145,602</td></tr><tr><td>HIM Business School</td><td>0</td><td>15,000–30,000</td><td>139,552–154,552</td></tr><tr><td>César Ritz Colleges</td><td>0</td><td>20,000–40,000</td><td>182,052–202,052</td></tr><tr><td>SHMS</td><td>0</td><td>15,000–30,000</td><td>194,252–209,252</td></tr><tr><td>Glion</td><td>12,600</td><td>20,000–40,000</td><td>257,527–277,527</td></tr><tr><td>Les Roches</td><td>12,640</td><td>20,000–40,000</td><td>251,792–271,792</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
<p><strong>Family net cost = school charges + unlisted housing and living costs − awarded scholarships − net internship savings.</strong> Room selection, travel, holiday arrangements and placement location turn this model into a personal budget. LEP can first clarify entry credits and scholarship eligibility with the school.</p>

<h2>Prepare the initial payment before future earnings arrive</h2>
<p>For the 2027 opening stage, Glion’s listed items and application fee total about 55,875, plus a refundable 1,250 deposit. At Les Roches, the sum of the components is about 50,450, plus a refundable 1,000 deposit. A refundable deposit is cash to prepare rather than final consumption if its return conditions are met. Internship income arrives later.</p>
<p>IMI merits attention for academic learning and a lower cost entry point. SEG schools should be compared by business, hotel, design or culinary direction. Glion and Les Roches require a judgement about the courses, campuses and career resources the student will actually use. More spending should correspond to a concrete benefit.</p>
