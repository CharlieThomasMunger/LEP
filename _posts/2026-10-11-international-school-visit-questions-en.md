## Return with a decision, not only photographs

A family's time in Europe is valuable. Attractive grounds and welcoming staff are useful impressions, but parents still need to understand entry level, learning support and everyday arrangements.

LEP starts with age, curriculum, English, subjects, interests and whether parents will relocate. These details shape which schools are worth visiting and which discussions matter.

## Use our fieldwork to narrow the trip

Chantemerle's smaller residential setting and Saint-Charles's combination of courses and facilities raise different questions. Eerde offers an English-medium boarding route in the Netherlands. Luxembourg day-school decisions also involve living arrangements beyond the campus.

Our [visit records](/en/fieldwork/) let families examine that background before travelling. We can then discuss two or three meaningfully different options rather than filling an itinerary with similar tours.

## Prepare a student-specific conversation

Language concerns need discussion alongside the intended course. Riding or another established interest requires a workable activity, travel, study and boarding schedule. For a first-time boarder, settling-in support and parent communication deserve attention.

For senior students, the availability of a qualification is only the starting point. LEP brings the student's records and intended subjects into the discussion to clarify entry and progression.

## Connect what we have seen with what the child will use

Saint-Charles's accommodation and facilities impressed me during the visit. The next questions concern the particular student: year group, residence and regular participation. At Chantemerle, a welcoming atmosphere must similarly be considered alongside course needs.

LEP's role is to translate observations and school responses into understandable choices for a family, rather than leaving parents to reconcile several brochures alone.

## Carry the visit forward

Afterwards, organise the likely entry point, support discussed, outstanding school questions and the accommodation-and-fee picture. Include the student's reactions alongside parents' observations.

The next step may be further records, an assessment or an application. If a school is unsuitable, the visit should still narrow the search.

Send LEP possible travel dates, school year, English level, preferred countries and the decisions you want the trip to resolve. We can discuss worthwhile visits and proceed around school availability. [Contact LEP](/en/families/).
