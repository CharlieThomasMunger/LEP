<h2 id="section-1">Why include Saint-Charles in a riding family’s shortlist?</h2>
<p>Saint-Charles International School is in Porrentruy, in French-speaking Switzerland. It is first a school with academic and boarding provision. Its current sports information includes horse riding, giving families a starting point for discussing how an existing interest might continue.</p><figure class="article-photo"><img src="/assets/swiss/sc-fold-riding.jpg" width="1200" height="1600" alt="Indoor riding arena visited during LEP’s Saint-Charles visit." loading="lazy" decoding="async"><figcaption>Indoor riding arena visited during LEP’s Saint-Charles visit.</figcaption></figure><p>I saw this arena during the visit, alongside the school’s learning and living environment. Seeing facilities is valuable; it does not by itself establish each student’s coach, training frequency or competition programme.</p>

<h2 id="section-2">A beginner and an established competitor need different answers</h2>
<p class="meta">On a small screen, scroll horizontally to see the full table.</p><div class="table-scroll" tabindex="0" role="region" aria-label="Experience / First questions"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Experience</th><th scope="col">First questions</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>New or recreational rider</td><td>Appropriate lessons, horses and supervision within the activity timetable.</td></tr><tr><td>Regular rider</td><td>A sustainable term-time frequency, riding time, coach and transport.</td></tr><tr><td>Competition ambitions</td><td>Assessment by the relevant coach and a specific plan for horses, events, accompaniment and academics.</td></tr></tbody></table></div><p>Families seeking intensive training, a competition season or arrangements for their own horse should make those needs explicit. LEP will take them to the school for an individual response. They should not be assumed to be included in a standard boarding package.</p>

<h2 id="section-3">Choose the academic direction first</h2>
<p>Saint-Charles offers an English-language IB direction and a separate Swiss curriculum route. Language preparation, placement and graduation timing differ. Riding facilities should complement a suitable education, rather than determine it before those questions have been answered.</p><p>A family considering international university options can explore the IB, while the Swiss route requires its own language and progression assessment. Students with substantial training demands should check how schoolwork, assessments and travel will coexist. Read our <a href="/en/insights/equestrian-training-ib-university/">guide to training and university planning</a>.</p><figure class="article-photo"><img src="/assets/swiss/sc16.jpg" width="1600" height="1067" alt="Saint-Charles campus and gardens. LEP visit photograph." loading="lazy" decoding="async"><figcaption>Saint-Charles campus and gardens. LEP visit photograph.</figcaption></figure>

<h2 id="section-4">Connect boarding care with time at the stable</h2>
<p>The school offers seven-day boarding. Its accommodation, fitness and swimming facilities made an impression on me during the visit. Good living facilities and an agreed arrangement for off-campus training are, however, separate practical questions.</p><figure class="article-photo"><img src="/assets/swiss/sc-fold-bedroom.jpg" width="1200" height="1600" alt="Student accommodation seen during the LEP visit to Saint-Charles." loading="lazy" decoding="async"><figcaption>Student accommodation seen during the LEP visit to Saint-Charles.</figcaption></figure><p>Map out a training day and an event day: permission, transport, accompaniment, return time and missed meals or study. When parents are abroad, responsibility needs to be attached to an identifiable role, not a general promise of care.</p>

<h2 id="section-5">Budget beyond tuition and boarding</h2>
<p>The 2026/27 seven-day boarding fee sheet gives CHF 92,500 for IB Diploma tuition and basic board. Optional and off-campus sports expenses are separate items. First-year charges, insurance, deposits and account advances also need to be distinguished; the <a href="/en/insights/equestrian-boarding-school-costs/">budget guide</a> explains the structure.</p>
