## Three pathways, three different interests

Hospitality, event and design management share foundations in hospitality and business before becoming more specialised. Students do not need to decide their entire career on arrival, but they should understand the differences.

| Undergraduate pathway | Examples of course content | What I discuss with a student |
|---|---|---|
| Hospitality Management | Rooms and food service, revenue management, hotel strategy, resort and spa operations | Willingness to learn through operations and balance guests, colleagues and costs |
| Event Management | Event concepts, project organisation, banqueting, planning and delivery | Interest in organising people and managing budgets, schedules and details |
| Design Management | Hotel and restaurant design, visual communication, AutoCAD and design innovation | Interest in spaces and guest experiences, and in turning aesthetics into workable operations |

Design management is easily misunderstood. It sits within hospitality and commercial spaces: an attractive lobby, restaurant or room must also work for guests and staff. This pathway is particularly relevant to students who want to connect design, business and service.

## From serving guests and keeping accounts to understanding hotel profitability

Early study includes food and beverage theory and practice, cost control, wine, accounting, rooms operations, languages and professional preparation. Later subjects include data-informed decisions, people management, brand marketing, research and business planning.

**Service training deserves to be taken seriously.** Someone preparing to manage a team needs to understand what happens in housekeeping, restaurants and reception. Internships bring costs, service and staffing into focus; students can then return to class with real questions.

The **2.5–3-year route** includes the ES professional diploma and progression to bachelor-level study. Families planning a full bachelor's degree must include the final stage, rather than treating an intermediate diploma as the completed degree.

## Which degrees and diplomas can students earn?

Students completing the corresponding programmes can earn the Swiss Advanced Federal Diploma of Higher Education in hospitality management, the relevant SHMS bachelor's degree and a BA (Hons) from the University of Derby in the UK.

The ES diploma is a Swiss higher professional education diploma; a BA is a bachelor's degree. The diploma and degrees correspond to different stages of study. If a student plans to continue to a master's, we consider the awarding institution, results and the requirements of the intended postgraduate programme.

SHMS is **fourth in the QS 2026 Hospitality & Leisure Management subject ranking**. This is a subject ranking, not fourth place among universities overall. Rankings help families become familiar with a school; the curriculum and practical experience explain what a student can learn there.

## What does the 2027 undergraduate route cost?

The 2027 fee schedule lists **CHF 186,500** for the full undergraduate route.

| Item | CHF |
|---|---:|
| Tuition | 130,700 |
| Accommodation during the periods covered by the schedule | 14,800 |
| Meals during the periods covered by the schedule | 12,400 |
| Infrastructure and services | 28,600 |
| **Total** | **186,500** |
| Additional deposit | 2,000 |

These figures are before scholarships. Accommodation and meals cover the study periods specified in the schedule; holidays, living costs during internships and personal spending need separate provision. Families paying in another currency should budget using the exchange rate when payment is due.
