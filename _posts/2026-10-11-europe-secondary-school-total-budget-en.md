<h2>My discussion order for a RMB 500,000 family</h2><p>For English-medium Luxembourg schools without accompanying parents, begin with course fit at OTR, St George’s or ISL and reserve funds for local services and entry costs. For Swiss IB boarding, explore Altdorf’s course position and actual quote.</p><p>Chantemerle’s English A Level route and Eerde’s IGCSE/DP base figures already exceed this illustrative annual budget. They become meaningful discussions where the family can increase the amount, including initial and holiday costs.</p>
<h2>Connect extra spending to the child’s needs</h2><p>I am James Liu. Saint-Charles’s residences, pool, riding and campus resources impressed me, while Chantemerle offers another small and welcoming environment. A Luxembourg host household also gives a different daily experience from Dutch or Swiss campus boarding.</p><p>Some students value family routines, others living with peers; some are ready for their course, others need preparation. Extra spending should solve an important need for this child.</p>
<h2>Cash at entry and the whole education stage</h2><p>Eerde’s listed upper-secondary first cash is EUR 68,000, including EUR 3,650 registration and EUR 2,750 deposit. Saint-Charles DP’s under-18 entry example is CHF 98,300 in listed spending and CHF 110,300 after deposit and personal-account advance.</p><p>These illustrate why initial cash differs from recurring expense. Later years, fee changes and support must also be planned. A sustainable route is more useful than a first year that consumes all available funds.</p>
