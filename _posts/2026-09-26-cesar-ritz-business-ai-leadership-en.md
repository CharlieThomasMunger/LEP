**By James Liu | Lux Education Partners | Updated 26 September 2026**

**César Ritz Colleges Switzerland (CRCS) deserves attention if a student wants to manage a business, join a family enterprise or become an entrepreneur. Its strengths include specialised business pathways, an emphasis on data and management analysis, and Swiss federal institutional accreditation.**

Within SEG, SHMS focuses on hospitality, events and design; HIM combines a three-year business degree with three internships; Culinary Arts Academy Switzerland specialises in culinary arts and food business. **CRCS is particularly relevant to students who want to learn how to run a business through experience in service industries.**

## Two undergraduate routes: business management or applied AI

| Degree | Main focus | Who should consider it? |
|---|---|---|
| BSc in Hospitality Business Management | Operations, finance, marketing, data analysis and strategy | Students interested in management, financial analysis, entrepreneurship or a family business |
| BSc in Applied AI for Hospitality Business Management | Using AI and data to improve customer experience, revenue and business processes | Students interested in applying technology to business problems |

**The business management degree offers six specialisations: Executive Leadership, Marketing and Communication, International Finance, Business Analytics, Digital Strategy and Entrepreneurship.** Applied AI is a separate bachelor's programme, with its first intake planned for **October 2027**.

Both complete undergraduate routes take four years and include two internships. The first year develops foundations in service operations, languages and professional practice before the main business curriculum. Students with relevant study or work experience can ask LEP to arrange an assessment by the school for a possible first-year exemption.

## A 15-month MSc in Leadership with three specialisations

**The MSc in Leadership takes 15 months** and offers:

- **Hospitality and Tourism Management:** management of hotels, tourism and service businesses.
- **Finance and Wealth Management:** finance and wealth management.
- **Luxury Brand Management:** brand management and high-end customer experience.

The course combines leadership, finance, marketing and strategy, including a dissertation stage. It suits graduates who want to develop their business management knowledge. Students from families running hotels, restaurants or other service businesses can also bring real company questions into their project work.

## Swiss accreditation and plans after graduation

**CRCS holds institutional accreditation under Switzerland's federal legal framework as a University of Applied Sciences Institute.** It also appears on the university list for Hong Kong's Top Talent Pass Scheme. Families considering that route need to check the bachelor's degree and the relevant application category.

Eligible graduates from outside the EU/EFTA can remain in Switzerland for six months after graduation to seek work related to their studies; employment still requires the appropriate permission. For students who hope to work in Europe, LEP considers **study, internships, employment recommendations and residence arrangements together**.
