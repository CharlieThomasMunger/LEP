## Why I include CRCS in a business-school discussion

When I visit a Swiss hospitality school, I want to understand how students learn to run a business. César Ritz Colleges Switzerland (CRCS) connects service operations with finance, marketing, data and strategy. For students considering international service companies, a family business or entrepreneurship, that combination deserves a closer look.

I visited CRCS and met its head during my October 2026 Swiss trip. LEP works directly with CRCS, bringing families’ questions into programme selection, applications and scholarship discussions. Our task is to identify a course that fits the student and turn the school’s responses into a practical enrolment plan.

## A head of school who made a memorable impression

The head was **warm, humorous, engaging and keen to talk in depth**. We took photographs and recorded an interview. Her approachable manner made the conversation natural and contributed to my positive impression of the school.

She later arranged for a young Italian guide to show us around. He was friendly, talkative and had visited China, which made for an easy conversation. During the changeover between classes, students gathered and chatted together. The head’s warmth, our guide’s enthusiasm and the lively student atmosphere formed a coherent picture of campus life.

## Living and studying in Brig

Brig felt like a pleasant town. The accommodation and teaching spaces we visited were approximately five minutes apart on foot. The buildings did not feel brand new, but the teaching areas and surroundings were clean and everyday life looked convenient.

LEP discusses the actual residence, room type, meals and walking route alongside courses and fees. These details matter to the daily routine of an undergraduate living away from home.

## Comparing the bachelor’s, applied AI and master’s routes

| Programme | Published structure | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| BSc in Hospitality Business Management | Full four-year route, 180 ECTS, two internships | Business operations, finance, marketing, data and services |
| BSc in Applied AI for Hospitality Business Management | Full four-year route, 180 ECTS, two internships; first intake October 2027 | Applying AI to revenue, customer experience and processes |
| MSc in Leadership | 15 months, 90 ECTS | Management development after a bachelor’s degree |

The business bachelor moves from an industry foundation year into business studies. The 2027 material lists six directions: executive leadership, marketing and communication, international finance, business analytics, digital strategy and entrepreneurship. LEP relates those choices to existing subjects and interests.

The new AI route merits a separate discussion: [how CRCS applied AI differs from computer science](/en/insights/cesar-ritz-applied-ai-degree/). Its first intake is in October 2027, so the curriculum and project design are the basis for evaluating it.

The MSc offers hospitality and tourism management, finance and wealth management, and luxury brand management. Its current structure comprises three on-campus terms and two off-campus terms, including an integrated leadership and management project or an entrepreneurship accelerator, with an optional global internship.

## Qualifications and English preparation

Undergraduate entry accepts secondary-school qualifications including a high-school diploma, IB and A Levels or equivalents. IELTS Academic is listed at **5.5 overall, with no component below 4.5**. LEP helps establish the correct entry point for the applicant’s school or previous university background.

The master’s normally requires a business-related bachelor’s degree. Other disciplines may be assessed individually, potentially with foundation study. IELTS Academic is **6.0 overall, with no component below 5.5**. Recognition of prior learning or experience is considered by the school.

LEP organises transcripts, course information and language evidence, then discusses entry stage, preparation, intake and scholarships with the school.

## 2027 tuition, accommodation and meals

These amounts, in Swiss francs, come from the school’s 2027 schedule and cover the **listed school charges for the complete programme**.

| Component | Hospitality Business bachelor: full four-year route | Leadership MSc: 15 months |
|---|---:|---:|
| Tuition | 88,000 | 35,500 |
| Accommodation in specified on-campus terms | 27,900 | 9,300 |
| Meals in specified on-campus terms | 13,500 | 4,500 |
| Infrastructure and services | 39,900 | 14,400 |
| **Total school charges (CHF)** | **169,300** | **63,700** |

A **CHF 2,000 deposit** is separate, and individual scholarships have not been deducted. LEP obtains a programme-specific quote for Applied AI rather than assuming the same price.

The schedule includes a deluxe single room and **weekday lunch and dinner** during the specified campus terms. Kitchens are available for weekend use. Christmas and inter-term accommodation is additional, with a listed booking price of **CHF 430 per week**. Housing and meals during internships or other off-campus periods, travel and personal expenses belong in the remaining budget.

LEP maps those costs onto the student’s campus, holiday and internship calendar to make payment timing clear.

## Turning internships into career preparation

The bachelor includes two internships. Career services provide CV, interview and career support, with recruitment events connecting students and employers. I focus on the work a student does, the feedback received and the next role that experience prepares them for.

An initial direction—operations, sales and marketing, revenue and data, or another service business—helps choose a useful role and location. Language development belongs in that plan.

CRCS holds Swiss institutional accreditation as a University of Applied Sciences Institute. For non-EU/EFTA students seeking Swiss employment after a Swiss higher-education qualification, SEM describes a six-month period to seek suitable work and rules for roles of significant scientific or economic interest. The appropriate work permission is part of the process. LEP considers the intended occupation, languages, internships and eligibility together.

## Who should explore CRCS with LEP?

Students interested in people, business and service, and willing to engage with finance and data, have reasons to consider CRCS. A family hotel, restaurant or other service venture can provide concrete goals for the studies. For a student targeting software development or computing research, we would compare the actual modules against computer-science alternatives.

For a European career ambition, we identify the industry and country early. Programme fit, family investment and future development should inform the same decision.
