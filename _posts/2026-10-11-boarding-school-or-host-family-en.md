<h2>Start with life after school</h2>
<p>How does the child relate to other people, express difficulties and respond to group activities? Which concerns matter most to the parents? The accommodation needs to support the curriculum and the child’s personality.</p>

<h2>Compare the same costs</h2>
<p>The following starts with annual secondary-school tuition and main accommodation/meals. Luxembourg uses 2026/27 English-language upper-secondary references plus about €25,000 for host-family board. Swiss figures use the relevant seven-day boarding schedules. Wikon remains a school-visit budget reference pending an itemised offer.</p>

<p>For scale only, using illustrative rates of €1 = CNY 8.5 and CHF 1 = CNY 9 gives about CNY 357,000–418,000 for the Luxembourg base and CNY 495,000–833,000 across the Swiss references. These are not current exchange quotes. Refundable deposits need cash but are not repeated annual consumption when returned.</p>
<p>The difference between two headline numbers is not yet the family’s saving. Add the services, activities, holidays and travel the child needs. LEP explains the budget alongside the corresponding care.</p>

<h2>What the visits changed in my judgement</h2>
<p>Wikon stood out for refreshed space and value, while its current on-site sports provision needs matching to the child’s interests. ENSR impressed me with order, cleanliness and renovated boarding. Lémania offers an urban setting using city resources. Chantemerle’s smaller atmosphere and Saint-Charles’ facilities suit different families.</p>
<p>For a Luxembourg host family, matching and follow-up are central. The same school can be experienced differently depending on home routines, food, study space and communication. LEP knows local families and works with the host-family association on continuing supervision.</p>
<p>We consider commuting distance, compatibility and local reputation, with supervision and coordination reflected in the service agreement. If the arrangement needs changing, LEP can coordinate another family and temporary accommodation. If the school also needs reconsideration, our local understanding and contacts allow support to continue.</p>

<h2>Build a realistic shortlist</h2>
<p>First establish the curriculum the child can join, then living arrangements and the full budget. A pupil already studying A Level should preserve course continuity; a student focused on Swiss universities should examine Maturité and IB requirements early.</p>
<p>LEP’s current Luxembourg private-school route serves children over 11 without requiring parents to relocate first. Suitable host-family arrangements are an essential prerequisite. Families can bring us the child’s circumstances rather than assemble the school and accommodation plan alone.</p>
<p>After the initial discussion, we narrow the routes and progress school contact, budget and accommodation. The result should be a few options the family can meaningfully pursue.</p>
