**If September 2027 is your intended start date at Glion or Les Roches, the right moment to organise your application is now.** That is particularly true if you intend to use the Saudi government's Excellence Track, which places a formal, unconditional admission letter at the centre of the process.

An effective timeline does more than put months on a calendar. It connects the dates when your school results, English certificate, Swiss admissions review, scholarship application and travel preparations can actually happen.

LEP can coordinate the school side of that journey with you.

## Why does a Saudi scholarship change the admissions timeline?

The Ministry of Education's published Excellence Track criteria call for a **direct, unconditional offer** for an eligible institution, specialisation and degree. The formal letter should identify the school, specialisation and programme start date.

Glion currently lists **September 2027** as a start date for its International Hospitality Business bachelor. Les Roches describes **spring and fall intakes** for its Global Hospitality Management bachelor. Your individual timetable will depend on the specific course, Swiss campus, qualification and admissions arrangements.

The practical implication is simple: prepare for the school's assessment **before** you need the final letter for the scholarship application. If you are awaiting a diploma or final A-level/IB results, that date becomes part of the planning from day one.

## A month-by-month planning framework for September 2027

This is **LEP's suggested working schedule**, designed to show what to begin and when. It is not a list of official school or Saudi ministry deadlines.

| Period | Your key task | What LEP can coordinate |
|---|---|---|
| **Nov–Dec 2026** | Share your current education, grades, expected graduation, English results and intended degree. | Evaluate Glion and Les Roches routes; identify any urgent admissions or scholarship-document questions. |
| **Jan–Feb 2027** | Prepare the application file and complete suitable school submission steps. | Liaise with admissions, check document completeness and follow up on assessments. |
| **Mar–Apr 2027** | Respond to interviews, assessments or requests for updated evidence. | Track school correspondence and organise a practical response to any offer conditions. |
| **May–Jun 2027** | Aim to complete as much of the formal admissions work as your academic calendar permits; check the current scholarship window. | Review the proposed degree, campus and start date on the offer; connect the remaining school tasks to funding timing. |
| **Jul–Aug 2027** | Supply final school results where applicable; complete funding, visa and enrolment arrangements as dates permit. | Coordinate school-side follow-up, accommodation queries and outstanding admission documents. |
| **Sep 2027** | Begin your confirmed programme. | Help with remaining school contact and practical arrival questions within the agreed support scope. |

Some school-leaving results are only issued in summer. An applicant in this position needs a schedule built around **their actual result-release date**, rather than an artificial promise that every document can be final by May.

Swiss entry-visa and accommodation arrangements also deserve time. A school offer arriving just before departure leaves a very narrow margin for everything else.

## Where do you apply for the Saudi scholarship?

The Saudi Ministry of Education now directs new scholarship submissions to **Qubool (منصة قبول)** in its [scholarship application service](https://moe.gov.sa/ar/services/unified-services-guide/ksp). The [Safeer portal](https://safeer2.moe.gov.sa/Portal/Home/Index) says most new applications have moved to Qubool while previous cases can still be followed through Safeer.

The public Excellence Track information available today includes some earlier academic-year references. Before you commit to a date, check the **2027 application window and current eligible university/programme list** in the ministry's latest notices.

For students, the most useful division of work is straightforward: use the government platform for the scholarship application, while LEP helps organise the **Glion or Les Roches admissions work and school documentation** that needs to be ready for it.

## What usually slows down a formal offer?

It is often something small enough to address early, yet significant enough to delay a decision:

- An IELTS certificate that needs a closer check for component scores or age.
- An academic transcript that requires clarification or updated results.
- A final school diploma scheduled to be issued later in the year.
- A conditional offer with several outstanding items and no clear follow-up calendar.
- A programme or campus description that needs to match the scholarship documents precisely.

LEP can help turn these into a manageable action list and communicate with the admissions team. If you already have an offer, you can start immediately with our [guide to progressing an unconditional Glion or Les Roches offer](/en/insights/unconditional-glion-les-roches-offer-saudi-scholarship/).
