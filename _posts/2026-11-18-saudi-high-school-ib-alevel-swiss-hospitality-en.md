For many students, the hardest part of applying to a Swiss hospitality school happens before the application form. You know your final examinations are approaching, but you are still waiting for a diploma. You have grades, perhaps a language certificate, and a preferred university. **What can you do now, rather than waiting until everything is final?**

Quite a lot. LEP helps Saudi students prepare an application that reflects the qualification they are actually studying, and then coordinates the relevant questions with **Glion or Les Roches**.

## Saudi high school, IB and A-levels: what changes?

These are three different academic routes, not three labels for the same application.

| Your qualification | What LEP checks first | What often determines the timing |
|---|---|---|
| Saudi secondary-school diploma | Curriculum, transcripts, the exact final certificate and any translation requirements | When the final diploma and verified results become available |
| International Baccalaureate (IB) | Predicted or final results, subject record and the form of the diploma | Release of the official final IB results |
| UK A-levels | Subjects, predicted or achieved grades and expected completion | Final A-level results and the school's admissions assessment |

Glion's [published bachelor criteria](https://www.glion.edu/apply-to-glion/application-process/admission-requirements/) expressly include eligible high-school diplomas, the IB Diploma and A-levels among the qualifications considered for its undergraduate route. Les Roches considers a range of academic and personal factors through its admissions process.

An international qualification does not automatically mean the same starting point at both institutions. We help you ask the *specific* question: which course stage should I apply for, and what evidence will admissions want to see?

## What documents should a Saudi school-leaver prepare?

Start with a small, well-organised application file:

1. Passport identification page and your full name as it appears on the passport.
2. Recent academic transcripts and a note of your curriculum.
3. Final school-leaving diploma, or current school evidence if you are still studying.
4. English-language results, including the date and each component score.
5. A brief CV and a convincing explanation of why hospitality business interests you.
6. Your desired course, Swiss campus and intake—particularly if scholarship timing is important.

Glion's [application process](https://www.glion.edu/apply-to-glion/application-process/) lists supporting documents such as a CV, transcripts, passport, motivation letter and post-study plan. The school may request additional material or an assessment. Les Roches will specify the documents appropriate to its programme and campus.

LEP turns that list into an actual submission plan. We help you identify what is ready today, what can be reviewed now and what must follow when final results arrive.

## My IELTS overall score is 5.5. Can I move forward?

For Glion's published Swiss International Hospitality Business bachelor requirements, an applicant who needs to provide IELTS should generally show **5.5 overall and at least 5.0 in each component**. Glion also specifies that English certificates must be issued within the 12 months before the programme starts, and it describes criteria related to prior English-medium schooling.

This is a good example of why a one-line score is not enough to plan an application. Send LEP all four results, the test date, your schooling history and your proposed intake. We can then discuss the appropriate evidence with the relevant admissions team.

We check the requirements for Les Roches separately against your chosen programme. That small distinction prevents avoidable delays caused by using one school's checklist for another.

## What if I receive a conditional offer?

A conditional offer can be a productive stage in an application. It tells you which items still need to be supplied or completed. The important move is to **turn each condition into a dated action**: final exam result, language evidence, document authentication, assessment or payment stage, as applicable.

For students planning to apply for Saudi Excellence Track funding, this is especially valuable because the ministry's published conditions call for a **direct, unconditional admission letter** to an eligible degree programme.

LEP helps track the outstanding items with the school, while building in enough time for the scholarship application. Our [step-by-step article on pursuing an unconditional offer](/en/insights/unconditional-glion-les-roches-offer-saudi-scholarship/) explains the process.
