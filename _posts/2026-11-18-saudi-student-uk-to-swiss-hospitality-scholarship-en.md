**You are Saudi, but your current school is in the United Kingdom. You want your next chapter to be in Switzerland.** Perhaps you are finishing A-levels, enrolled in a foundation programme or already one or two years into university. You have heard of Glion or Les Roches and want to understand how to make the move—particularly if a Saudi government scholarship is part of your plan.

The first useful step is to turn your UK study record into a clear question for Swiss admissions. LEP can help you do that.

## What route are you actually applying through?

Three Saudi students may all say *“I study in the UK”* and need three different admission conversations.

| Your situation in the UK | The admission question LEP will help you put to the school |
|---|---|
| Completing A-levels | Does my final secondary qualification support entry into the chosen bachelor's route, and when can the application be assessed? |
| Taking a foundation course | How does the institution evaluate this foundation programme alongside my original school qualification? |
| Already enrolled at university | Should I apply for a fresh bachelor's entry or request a review of possible direct entry or credit transfer? |

Glion describes [direct-entry and transfer assessment](https://www.glion.edu/apply-to-glion/application-process/transfer-and-direct-entries/) for relevant previous study or experience. Les Roches can consider the applicant's educational record and proposed programme through its admission process. An individual review makes the distinction clearer than a generic transfer checklist.

LEP helps assemble transcripts, course descriptions, credit information, current enrolment evidence and expected completion dates so the school can respond to your actual background.

## Does studying in England settle the English requirement?

It can provide valuable evidence, but the most useful starting point is the **exact programme requirement**.

Glion's published Swiss International Hospitality Business bachelor entry information specifies IELTS **5.5 overall and at least 5.0 per component** for applicants who must supply a test. It also refers to recent study at a school where English is the main language of instruction. Its London route has separate UKVI-related provisions.

If you have an IELTS UKVI score of 5.5, share the complete score report and date with LEP. Include the name of your UK school or university and how long you have studied there. We can bring this information together for the admissions team to review against your **Swiss** route.

This early conversation can clarify whether further evidence, a newer test or a different admission step needs attention.

## What happens to a Saudi scholarship when you change country or degree?

The Saudi Ministry of Education's published **Excellence Track** conditions include a direct, unconditional offer for an eligible institution and specialisation, full-time study and relevant country-of-study criteria.

For a UK student planning a Swiss degree, a useful application plan therefore has two columns:

1. **What the Swiss school can offer:** entry stage, transferable academic work where applicable, programme, campus and admission documentation.
2. **What the Saudi funding route requires:** eligible institution and degree, accepted place of study, admission letter and the applicant's scholarship status.

If you already receive funding for your current UK studies, include that information in your case review. If you are considering starting a new bachelor, make that clear from the outset. LEP helps you organise the questions and the documentation so that the school's proposed route can be checked against the relevant scholarship requirements.

Our separate [Swiss-campus and Saudi scholarship guide](/en/insights/saudi-scholarship-glion-les-roches-swiss-campus/) explains why the study location deserves special attention.

## Why talk to an adviser who has actually visited the schools?

I have visited both **Les Roches and Glion** in Switzerland, met school teams and looked closely at the experience students have beyond the brochure. For someone moving from the UK, I would consider not only the academic route but also the change in learning style, accommodation, practical training and future career direction.

LEP works locally in Europe and can coordinate the school enquiry and application as a coherent process. This is useful when your current studies, final exam dates and Swiss intake are running on different calendars.

You do not need a finished transfer plan before contacting us. Send:

- Your nationality and current UK school, university or foundation programme.
- Your transcript and, if relevant, course or credit descriptions.
- Your expected graduation or course-completion date.
- IELTS or other English evidence, including component scores and issue date.
- Your preferred Glion or Les Roches programme, Swiss campus and intake.
