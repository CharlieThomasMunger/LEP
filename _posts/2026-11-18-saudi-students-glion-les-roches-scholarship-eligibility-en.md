If you are a Saudi student who wants to study hospitality management in Switzerland, you may already be much closer to a workable plan than you think. The important question is not simply *“Which school is on the scholarship list?”* It is **“Which degree can I enter, at which campus, with which qualifications—and how do I get the right admission letter in time?”**

Those are exactly the questions Lux Education Partners (LEP) can help you work through with Glion and Les Roches.

## Are Glion and Les Roches recognised for Saudi hospitality scholarships?

The [Saudi Ministry of Education's additional university list](https://www.moe.gov.sa/ar/education/higher-education/additional-unis) names **Glion Institute of Higher Education** and **Les Roches Global Hospitality Education** under hospitality management. They are two particularly relevant Swiss routes for applicants exploring the **Excellence Track (Masar Al-Tamayuz)**.

The published Excellence Track conditions include Saudi nationality, admission to an eligible institution and specialisation, and a **direct, unconditional offer** for the specified degree. They also address full-time study and the country in which the institution is based.

For a September 2027 applicant, we would therefore check three things together:

1. The scholarship's university and specialisation list applicable to your application year.
2. Your proposed degree and **actual Swiss campus**, including any preparatory or cross-campus stages.
3. The documents and admissions steps needed to obtain an appropriate formal offer.

The ministry's accessible pages include information from earlier academic years. A 2027 applicant should work from the rules in force when the scholarship application opens.

At Glion, the published minimum starting age for the International Hospitality Business bachelor route is **17.5**, and recognised school-leaving qualifications include the IB Diploma, A-levels and eligible high-school diplomas. Les Roches reviews academic preparation, English and other application materials for the intended programme.

Your nationality establishes one part of a Saudi funding application; your individual academic record determines how to approach the school. LEP brings the two conversations into a single application plan.

## Why the exact Swiss study route matters

Glion offers hospitality and business education across more than one study location. Les Roches has campuses in Switzerland and other countries. For a government-funded student, the **name printed on the admission letter is only one part of the picture**: the degree, teaching location and study arrangement matter too.

This is a practical reason to involve LEP early. Rather than sending identical enquiries to several admissions inboxes, we help establish which programme and Swiss campus fit your goal, then ask the school the questions relevant to your case.
