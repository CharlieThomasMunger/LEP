<h2 id="section-1">What do the current school materials confirm?</h2>
<p>Eerde International Boarding School Netherlands is based in Ommen. Its equestrian brochure and current website describe coordination between academic work, training and competition, with an option to bring a horse. Pre-IB and the IB Diploma Programme are among its educational pathways.</p><p>That makes a combined school-and-training discussion possible from the outset. It does not establish a fixed number of lessons, an automatic event entry or the same timetable flexibility for every child.</p><figure class="article-photo"><img src="/assets/eerde/n7-4.webp" width="1600" height="1000" alt="School and riding schedules need to be considered together." loading="lazy" decoding="async"><figcaption>School and riding schedules need to be considered together.</figcaption></figure>

<h2 id="section-3">Why the boarding environment matters to riders</h2>
<p>During my visit I saw the accommodation and teaching environment. Historic buildings sit alongside modern teaching facilities. The school explained shared meals with staff and the expectation that students clear up after themselves. Those routines make boarding more tangible for parents who will remain overseas.</p><figure class="article-photo"><img src="/assets/eerde/eerde-fold-modern-teaching.jpg" width="1600" height="1200" alt="Historic buildings and modern teaching space at Eerde. LEP visit photograph." loading="lazy" decoding="async"><figcaption>Historic buildings and modern teaching space at Eerde. LEP visit photograph.</figcaption></figure><p>Training has to fit into that wider life: meals, study, friendships and rest. When discussing a visit, look beyond the arena and follow the child’s day through to bedtime. Check the individual age, course and residence conditions before treating a general boarding age as an admission entitlement.</p>

<h2 id="section-4">What should an experienced rider share?</h2>
<p>School reports and language information help assess academic placement. Recent full riding videos, competition results, weekly training and details of the horses ridden help the training provider understand sporting experience. A short highlight reel or the number of years spent riding is not a complete assessment.</p><p>If the child has limited experience with unfamiliar horses or competitions, say so. Bringing a horse is an option, not a requirement stated for every applicant. Availability and cost of a suitable local horse must be confirmed.</p>

<h2 id="section-5">Separate school fees from equestrian expenditure</h2>
<p>The school-provided 2026/27 fee booklet lists IB Diploma tuition plus standard full boarding at €61,600 per academic year. Do not treat this as an all-inclusive competitive riding price. Training, horses, care, transport and events need their own scope and quotation. See the <a href="/en/insights/equestrian-boarding-school-costs/">cost guide</a> for first-year payments and other items.</p>
