<h2>Compare the actual learning stage</h2><div class="table-scroll"><table><thead><tr><th>Question</th><th>International School Altdorf</th><th>Saint-Charles</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Upper-secondary route</td><td>IB Diploma Programme; IB career-related route</td><td>English Pre-DP and IB DP</td></tr><tr><td>Preparation</td><td>IGCSE and individual entry discussion</td><td>English Pre-DP; separate Swiss pathways</td></tr><tr><td>Main comparison</td><td>Course, support and sustainable budget</td><td>Course, accommodation and campus resources</td></tr><tr><td>Without accompanying parents</td><td>Boarding with a full calendar plan</td><td>Seven-day boarding with holiday planning</td></tr></tbody></table></div><p>IB DP is a two-year programme with six subjects and the TOK, EE and CAS core. IB CP combines academic subjects with career-related learning and is a different qualification route. LEP clarifies the programme and intended university use before application.</p><p>A child in lower secondary or the first year of Chinese high school may need academic-English and subject preparation. An older student needs a closer review of course continuity; being in Chinese Grade 11 does not automatically permit entry to the second DP year.</p>
<h2>Annual spending and first-entry cash</h2><p>Saint-Charles’s 2026/27 seven-day boarding schedule lists <strong>CHF 90,500 for English Pre-DP and CHF 92,500 for DP per school year</strong>. Seven-day describes the weekly boarding arrangement, not the length of the billing period.</p><p>For a new DP student under 18, application CHF 300, registration CHF 3,000 and scheduled insurance CHF 2,500 bring the listed expenditure to <strong>CHF 98,300</strong>. Add the CHF 5,000 deposit and CHF 7,000 personal-account advance for <strong>CHF 110,300 in initial cash</strong>. Advances differ from final spending. Where required, the listed additional language support adds CHF 12,000.</p><p>My earlier Altdorf screening reference is approximately <strong>CHF 50,000–55,000 per year</strong>. LEP obtains the relevant current course-and-boarding schedule and separates insurance, support and holidays before a final comparison. A planning reference and a dated official schedule serve different stages of the decision.</p>
<h2>Why Saint-Charles’s facilities impressed me</h2><p>At the Porrentruy school I saw the newer residences, gym space and strong campus facilities. Riding resources, a pool with adjustable depth and the town cinema within the school make its daily environment distinctive.</p><p>These resources matter if the child will use them. I would connect the extra budget with actual sport, living and activity needs rather than choose from attractive photographs alone.</p>
<h2>How I approach a budget-focused choice</h2><p>For Altdorf I first ask whether the course, English and academic support fit the student and whether the family can sustain the complete stage. A student with stable subject habits may benefit from focusing funds on English-medium school and university preparation.</p><p>Where campus activities and accommodation are major priorities and the full Saint-Charles budget is comfortable, both schools merit a discussion. The shortlist comes from the child’s situation.</p>
