For Families

Choosing a school in Luxembourg from abroad is hard: curricula differ, language tracks matter, and reliable local answers are scarce. We help international families understand the landscape and manage the practical steps — honestly, and without ever promising an admission.

School Search & Admissions Support

Realistic guidance on year-group fit, language tracks and curricula (IB, A-level, European sections), plus documentation-first coordination with the schools you apply to.

Host Family & Guardianship Coordination

Where a child studies in Luxembourg without a parent, we coordinate host family and guardianship arrangements in line with each school's policy — safeguarding-focused and documentation-first.

Settling In
