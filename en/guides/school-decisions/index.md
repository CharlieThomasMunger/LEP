## [How LEP matches a quiet or less independent teenager with a European school](/en/insights/boarding-school-readiness/)

LEP turns a student’s learning, language and daily support needs into specific discussions with schools such as Chantemerle, Saint-Charles and Eerde, alongside Luxembourg options. A personality label alone does not determine the shortlist.

## [School visits with LEP: turning a European trip into an admission decision](/en/insights/international-school-visit-questions/)

LEP uses the student’s background to shortlist visits, prepare school discussions and compare the resulting course, accommodation and fee information. Our visits and direct school communication connect a tour with an application plan.

## [Comparing European secondary-school budgets with LEP](/en/insights/europe-secondary-school-total-budget/)

LEP connects the student’s year, course and accommodation needs with school fees, holiday arrangements, additional support and local services. The goal is a sustainable school plan, not a ranking by advertised tuition.

## [Parents staying abroad: how LEP connects a child’s first term in Europe](/en/insights/first-term-overseas-school/)

LEP connects applications, accommodation, arrival handover and parent communication. Luxembourg families can discuss host-family and local coordination, while Swiss and Dutch boarding preparation identifies school contacts and practical routines.

## [A child moving without parents: boarding and host-family routes with LEP](/en/insights/boarding-school-or-host-family/)

LEP compares Luxembourg day schools with host families against Swiss and Dutch campus boarding. Curriculum, routines, parental plans and budget shape the route, followed by applications and the relevant accommodation coordination.

## [From Swiss summer camp to school choice: how LEP connects the steps](/en/insights/swiss-school-camp-before-boarding/)

LEP can explore short residential programmes at Chantemerle and Saint-Charles around a family’s longer-term school objectives, then connect observations with visits and applications.

## Discuss your child’s plans with LEP

Share age, current curriculum, English level, parental plans, intended start and annual budget.
