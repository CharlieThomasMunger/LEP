--- layout: fieldwork lang: en permalink: /en/fieldwork/ title: Fieldwork heading: Where our information comes from standfirst: The judgments on this site come from on-site visits, direct conversations with schools, and accumulated casework — not from information copied off the internet. description: School visits in Luxembourg and Switzerland by Lux Education Partners, and the evidence grading applied to every conclusion on this site. --- ## Why this page exists Study-abroad information is repeated widely online, but the source is rarely stated. Policies change, schools differ from one another, and a claim that was accurate three years ago may no longer hold. So every guide on this site marks the basis of its central answer: - **A — government regulation or official document** — written as "under the regulations…" - **B — written confirmation from a school or institution** — written as "the school currently confirms…" - **C — multiple observed cases in practice** — written as "in cases we have handled…" - **D — our own professional judgment** — written as "my judgment is…" **We do not present D as A.** This matters most where minors, guardianship, residence and school policy are involved. ## A visit is not an endorsement What follows records where we have been and what was discussed. **It does not mean any school endorses, authorises or recommends our services.** Admissions and partnership decisions rest entirely with the school. Unless stated otherwise, these are visits and conversations, not partnerships.
