--- layout: page lang: en permalink: /en/families/ title: For families heading: For families standfirst: One point of contact, from school selection through arrival. description: Support for international families placing a child in school in Luxembourg — selection, application, guardianship, host family, arrival. --- ## How it works 1. **First conversation** — your child's situation, timeline and budget, and which schools are realistic. 2. **Documentation** — files prepared to each school's requirements. 3. **Application and follow-up** — direct liaison with admissions, progress tracked, exceptions flagged immediately. 4. **Guardianship and accommodation** — where no parent is resident, coordinated in line with each school's policy. 5. **Arrival and beyond** — pre-arrival briefing and ongoing updates per the school's policy. ## What we do not do We do not promise admission. We do not decide on a school's behalf. We do not give legal or immigration advice — where residency matters arise, we will tell you what kind of professional you need. ## Contact contact@luxedupartners.lu
