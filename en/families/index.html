Lux Education Partners (LEP) is a Luxembourg-based European education services brand operated by Silver Bay Mountain Group S.à r.l., registered under RCS B293621. Its website is luxedupartners.lu.

LEP works directly with students and families on Luxembourg school applications and host-family arrangements, international boarding schools in the Netherlands and Switzerland, and Swiss undergraduate and postgraduate hospitality education. Support starts before arrival, with application documents, school liaison and coordination of documents required for student visa applications, and continues through the agreed arrival and local arrangements.

Before applying

You can contact LEP directly while your family is still abroad, including when parents will not accompany their child. We assess age, academic and language background, previous curriculum, budget and parental accompaniment.

Application documents and school liaison

We organise school reports and supporting documents against the school’s checklist, coordinate language versions and additional requirements, and liaise with admissions about assessments and interviews.

Student visa documentation

We help organise study-related documents and coordinate admission and accommodation evidence. Submission arrangements, residence routes and steps requiring the applicant’s personal involvement are confirmed for each case.

Accommodation and arrival

We coordinate suitable Luxembourg host-family arrangements; for Dutch and Swiss boarding schools, we clarify weekends, school holidays and guardianship separately.

Ongoing communication and future studies

We maintain the agreed liaison with the school, family and relevant local contacts, connecting this with curriculum choices and future study planning.

Schools decide admissions; the relevant authorities decide visa and residence applications. Before work begins, we agree the scope and fees, including what LEP handles, what the family must complete personally and where third parties are involved.

Share the student’s age, current class, languages and academic background, preferred start date, annual budget and whether a parent will accompany them.
