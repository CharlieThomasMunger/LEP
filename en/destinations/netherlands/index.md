## Eerde International Boarding School

Our current school focus in the Netherlands is Eerde International Boarding School in Ommen. We have established direct contact with its admissions team and are working on a school visit, student conversations and cooperation arrangements.

For families considering boarding education, we look beyond the campus to curriculum transitions, English preparation, adjustment to boarding and future study plans. Please share your child’s age, current year, curriculum and intended start date so we can discuss next steps using information from the school.
